Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Strategic Alliance for Medical Access (SAMA), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal analytical data to help us improve user experience and site navigation, as well as personal details (such as email addresses) voluntarily submitted through our contact channels.

2. Analytics & Cookies

We may use standard web analytics tools and cookies to measure general website performance and improve user experience. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options.

3. Log Files

We follow a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as part of hosting services' analytics.

4. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at contact@samamedical.org.
