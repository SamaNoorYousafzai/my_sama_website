Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

At Strategic Alliance for Medical Access (SAMA), accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by us and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal analytical data to help us improve user experience and site navigation.

2. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet. Visitors may choose to decline the use of DART cookies by visiting the Google ad and content network Privacy Policy at the following URL – https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Log Files

We follow a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. All hosting companies do this as part of hosting services' analytics.

4. Contact Information

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us.
