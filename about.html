About Strategic Alliance for Medical Access (SAMA)

Strategic Alliance for Medical Access (SAMA) is a Canadian nonprofit organization working to improve ethical healthcare access through education, scientific collaboration, regenerative medicine, advanced wound-care awareness, and community-centered initiatives.

Our Mission

Our mission is to expand equitable access to regenerative medicine and healthcare innovation through ethical practices, scientific collaboration, education, and community-centered initiatives.

Our Work

SAMA develops and supports ethical healthcare-access initiatives, professional education, scientific collaboration, and community-centered approaches to regenerative medicine and advanced wound-care awareness.

Information about current and developing programs, educational activities, partnerships, and impact documentation is available on our Regenerative Medicine Access page.
