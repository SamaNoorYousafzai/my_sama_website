About Us

Who we are

Strategic Alliance for Medical Access (SAMA) is a Canadian nonprofit organization advancing regenerative medicine, advanced wound care, and healthcare innovation through ethical partnerships, research, education, and humanitarian collaboration.

  • Why SAMA exists Too many patients still lack access to safe, ethical, and innovative care. We work to close that gap through science and partnership—not commercial pressure.
  • Canadian nonprofit identity Rooted in Canada’s values of public trust, equity, and responsible healthcare leadership, with a mandate that extends to global humanitarian need.
  • Leadership through collaboration We connect clinicians, researchers, universities, hospitals, and humanitarian organizations around shared ethical standards.

Our foundation

Mission, vision, and values

The principles that guide every partnership, program, and research conversation.

01 — Mission

Our Mission

To expand equitable access to regenerative medicine and healthcare innovation through ethical practices, scientific collaboration, education, and community-centered initiatives.

02 — Vision

Our Vision

A world where every patient—regardless of location or income—has access to safe, ethical, and innovative regenerative healthcare solutions.

03 — Values

Our Values

  • Scientific integrity
  • Ethical stewardship
  • Compassion & dignity
  • Transparent partnership
  • Equity of access
Impact areas

Where SAMA concentrates its work

Five interconnected focus areas that connect laboratory science with patients and communities who need care most.

Regenerative Medicine

Supporting ethical research pathways and clinical collaboration around emerging regenerative therapies and tissue-based innovation.

Advanced Wound Care

Expanding awareness and responsible access to advanced approaches for complex wounds in resource-limited and specialized settings.

Trauma & Burn Care

Building collaborative capacity for trauma-informed and burn-care pathways where humanitarian need and clinical complexity meet.

Healthcare Education

Sharing knowledge with clinicians, partners, and communities so innovation is understood, applied ethically, and sustained over time.

Humanitarian Medical Access

Partnering to extend ethical medical innovation to underserved and conflict-affected communities with dignity and accountability.

Ethical Stewardship

Upholding responsible standards for donation awareness, research collaboration, and patient-centered decision-making across all programs.
